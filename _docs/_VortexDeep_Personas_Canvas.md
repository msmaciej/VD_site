## 0. How to use this

The **Value Proposition Canvas** (Osterwalder / Strategyzer) has two halves that must *fit*:

| Customer Profile (right) | Value Map (left) |
|---|---|
| **Jobs** — what they are trying to get done | **Products & Services** — what you actually deliver |
| **Pains** — what annoys, blocks, costs them | **Pain Relievers** — how you remove that specific pain |
| **Gains** — what a good outcome looks like | **Gain Creators** — how you produce that specific gain |

**The rule:** every Pain Reliever must point at a named Pain. Every Gain Creator must point at a named Gain. If it points at nothing, cut it — it does not belong on the website either.

## 5. The interview — 20 minutes, in German, NO SELLING

Target: **8–10 conversations** in two weeks. Do not pitch. The moment it becomes a sales call, the data dies.

### Script

1. **Erzählen Sie mir von der letzten Woche — wo ist die Zeit tatsächlich hingegangen?**
2. **Welche Aufgabe haben Sie am meisten ungern gemacht?**
3. **Was haben Sie bereits versucht, um das zu lösen? Warum hat es aufgehört zu funktionieren?**
4. **Was passiert, wenn das morgen komplett ausfällt?**
5. **Was macht Ihnen Sorgen, wenn Sie an KI in Ihrem Betrieb denken?**
6. **Wer müsste sonst noch Ja sagen?**
7. **(immer!) Wen kennen Sie, der das gleiche Problem hat?**

### Rules

- **Write down their exact words.** Their sentences become your headlines. That is the entire deliverable.
- Never ask "would you buy this?" — people lie to be polite. Ask what they *did*, not what they *would* do.
- Never propose a solution during the call. If they ask for one, say you'll come back to them — that's your second meeting.
- Always ask for the referral. This is how a cold list becomes warm.

### Capture sheet (one per interview)

```
Firma / Branche / Grösse:
Datum / Kanal (Telefon, vor Ort):
Trigger — what set them off:
Job to be done (their words):
Top 3 pains (their words, verbatim):
What they already tried, and why it failed:
Their fear about AI (verbatim):
What a good outcome looks like (verbatim):
Who else decides:
Referral given? (name):
Would they look at a fixed-price pilot? Y / N / maybe
```
