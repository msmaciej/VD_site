# VortexDeep — Learning Path & Skill Roadmap

**Owner:** Maciek Szczech · VortexDeep · Wald ZH
**Date:** July 2026
**Purpose:** The sequence of Coursera specializations that take VortexDeep from
no-code delivery to building its own differentiated, privacy-preserving systems.

> **How to read this:** it's a ladder to climb *as client demand pulls you up it*,
> not a syllabus to finish before you begin. Phases 1–2 make you sellable;
> Phases 3–4 make you impossible to undercut on the things you actually promise.
> See `_VortexDeep_Personas_Canvas.md` — validate against a live customer before
> over-studying ahead of it.

---

## The core idea: two tracks, not one line

- **Track A — Delivery (Phases 1–2):** no-code / low-code. Gets to revenue fast.
  Handles the ~60–70% of SME work that is genuinely no-code.
- **Track B — Engineering (Phases 3–4):** into code. Lets you build document
  extraction (RAG), knowledge/SOPs and multi-area orchestration **yourself**,
  without routing a client's data through a third-party black box.

Start A first. Begin B once A is funding the time. They are complementary — A
makes you sellable, B protects the promise A is selling.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation: no-code, with ethics as the frame *(beginner)*

Ethics moved to the front deliberately: VD's whole pitch is data protection and
human-in-the-loop (revDSG, "nothing goes out without your approval"). It should
colour every build from day one, not close the path.

1. **LearnQuest — Ethics in the Age of AI**
2. **LearnQuest — No-Code AI for Business** — use-case spotting + business-process
   automation. Your 20 years of process/PM work pays off here; expect to move fast.
3. **LearnQuest — Build AI Apps with No-Code Tools** — assembling working automations.
4. **LearnQuest — AI Agents: Complete Beginner's Guide** — agent vs. prompt vs. chatbot.

## Phase 2 — Applied automation & orchestration *(beginner → low-code)*

5. **Edureka — Generative AI for Automation** — the bridge. Prompt engineering,
   workflow design, Power Automate, tool calling, multi-agent workflows.
   *(Beginner-level and confirmed **not** a difficulty spike — position 4 or 5 both fine.)*
6. **LearnQuest — Build AI Agents with Practical App Design** — pulls it into
   designed, client-facing agents; closes out the no-code capability.

**→ END OF THE "START EARNING" LINE.** After Phase 2 you can sell and deliver the
no-code share of the work.

## Phase 3 — Into code: build it yourself *(beginner → intermediate)*

7. **Vanderbilt — Generative AI Software Engineering** — first real code: Python
   agents that process data and analyse documents, plus custom GPTs.
   *(Note: Vanderbilt's opening no-code course is intentionally **dropped** — Phase 1
   already covers that ground; don't pay for the overlap.)*
8. **Vanderbilt — AI Agents and Agentic AI in Python** — agents from the ground up
   (agent loops, tools, multi-agent). Now you understand what the no-code tools
   were quietly doing for you.

## Phase 4 — Production depth: the differentiators *(advanced)*

9. **IBM — RAG and Agentic AI Professional Certificate** — the capstone.
   - RAG + vector databases **=** your document extraction and Knowledge/SOPs work.
   - LangGraph / CrewAI / MCP **=** your "one smooth reply across several areas."
   - This is the rung that lets you build the flagship **Treuhand** case study
     yourself, without a third party touching client data — the whole reason it
     belongs at the top of *your* path specifically.
   - *Optional reinforcement:* **Coursera — Hands-on Agentic AI**, for its
     governance/compliance angle (loops the engineering back to the no-black-box promise).

## Optional vertical add-ons — only when a real client pulls you there

- **Chatbots** → IBM *Building AI Powered Chatbots Without Programming* (no-code,
  watsonx). If you do Phase 4, you get RAG chatbots for free — this becomes mostly packaging.
- **Customer-service GenAI** → Edureka *Mastering Generative AI for Customer Service
  Excellence* (sentiment analysis, inquiry classification, email automation — maps
  straight onto "Customer Support" and "Feedback & Reviews").
- **Sentiment / market research** → DeepLearning.AI *Natural Language Processing
  Specialization* (sentiment through to transformers).
- **FX / investment** → Google Cloud + NYIF *Machine Learning for Trading*.
  A separate, higher-risk, regulated business line — **not** an adjacent branch.

## Timing & the one honest caveat

- Phases 1–2: weeks, not months, given your background.
- Phase 3: start once no-code deals fund the time.
- Phase 4: the deep investment — make it **against a real client problem**, not before one.
- Cost note: nearly all of these sit under a single **Coursera Plus** subscription,
  so the real cost is time, not enrolments. Re-check levels/availability on Coursera
  directly before enrolling — the catalog shifts.
